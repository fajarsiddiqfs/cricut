Hi, I'm Fajar Siddiq.Follow me on @fajarsiddiq TikTok, Instagram, YouTube

I'm a Business Owner & Founder of Euphorya from Singapore and currently based in Malaysia. I'm building 5+ Brands using Cricut and I'm an Influencer/KOL for @cricut_sgmy “Cricut – Singapore + Malaysia”. I've also collaborated with Cricut Ambassadors and other Business Owners & Founders.

I started with Cricut as a hobby making stickers and vinyl pieces plus iron-on t-shirts and small gifts at home. Now I've turned that same machine into a way to build real brands and sell what I make. Everything on this website comes from that hands-on experience. My videos show the exact projects I made and list the machines, tools and materials I used so you can find and buy them in one tap. I also share workshops, Cricut socials, meet-ups, tips and fresh project ideas with the maker community. Whether you're picking up your first Cricut or ready to sell to customers in Malaysia and Singapore my goal is to keep it simple, honest and beginner-friendly so you can create with confidence and grow at your own pace.
