Warranty & Replacement Process
  1. Contact Cricut Member CareReport the issue by phone, email or live chat. They will check if your machine is covered.
  2. Get a Warranty Case Ticket IDIf covered, you will receive a case Ticket ID and the faulty machine will be deactivated.
  3. Contact Cricut MalaysiaMessage them on Shopee or TikTok Shop with your case Ticket ID.
  4. Return the MachineFollow the return instructions. Once approved, a replacement will be arranged (subject to warranty terms).

No reply from Shopee?

Email at service@banleong.com.my / support@cricut.com with:
